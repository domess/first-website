My background is a rather unusual mix of mathematical modelling, visual computing, physics and economics. During my PhD, I worked with 3D scanning of seal skulls, turning physical specimens into high-resolution 3D models and developing workflows to process the scans, analyse 3D coordinates and validate digital measurement methods. I have worked with various types of data, from images and 3D data to experimental measurements and surveys.
Visualisation was for a long time simply part of doing research. Recently, I discovered the much broader field of data visualisatios and the community around it, and I found that surprisingly exciting! Since then, I have been exploring dataviz more deliberately, learning new tools and techniques, and experimenting with my own projects.

I am currently exploring D3 through an online course.
